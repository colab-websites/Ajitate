# Ajitate design direction

Preserve Sergio's established red-and-black identity, original hero film, supplied logo, condensed display typography and glass navigation. Refine the existing React/Vite site; do not replace its visual direction.

- Audience: Spanish-speaking restaurant visitors in Cuenca. Primary action: explore the menu, assemble an order and review a copyable inquiry.
- Colors: brand red #ff3038, canvas #000000, surface #121010, warm secondary surface #1b1113, body #ffffff, secondary text #d5cccc. Red accents distinguish actions and brand headings.
- Type: Bebas Neue for expressive headings, Prompt for readable descriptions and controls. Price digits use tabular spacing.
- Layout: keep the cinematic, asymmetric desktop hero. Provide an obvious menu action below the introduction. Mobile combines a shorter film, readable headline and immediate menu action. Content is limited to a 1180px grid.
- Menu: quiet solid cards let food imagery lead. A compact combo banner leaves more room for products. Two special-edition items share a balanced two-column row. Phone categories scroll horizontally; search remains fully visible.
- Photography: reuse supplied restaurant photographs and existing illustrative food imagery. Identify menu imagery as illustrative; do not imply exact servings. No additional generated media required.
- Interaction: native links/buttons/dialog; visible focus, 44px controls, Escape dismissal for navigation, reduced-motion still video, explicit play/pause. Do not delay headline visibility on video/network readiness.
- Facts: preserve source menu prices, hours, location and history. The checkout prepares an inquiry only; it does not send an order, accept payment or guarantee availability. Publishing requires a separate instruction.
